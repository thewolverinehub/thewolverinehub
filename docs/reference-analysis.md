## 1. lokalankafitness.com

### Overview
A full-service gym and class hub in Ahangama, Sri Lanka. Gym + class model with accommodation as a secondary offering. Core audience: travelling fitness enthusiasts, digital nomads, expats, and local regulars.

### Navigation & Header
- Transparent header: Logo · "Train" dropdown · "Discover" dropdown · Testimonials · Contact · Log in
- Social icons: Instagram, Facebook, YouTube
- No announcement bar or scroll-behaviour changes noted

### Home Page Flow (in order)
1. **Hero** — tagline "Train Hard. Feel Good. Deep in the Jungle." + "View Schedule" CTA
2. **Stat counters** — 200+ Clients/Week · 15 Years Experience · 3,000+ Sessions Coached
3. **Class grid** — 24 cards (name, duration, 1-line description, "View Details")
4. **Member app features** — online booking, class packs/credits, QR check-in, gamification (levels, badges, streaks)
5. **Story triptych** — "Built on Grit" (Our Story) · "Heart of Ahangama" (Location, 92% results stat) · "100% Real Results" (Vibe/philosophy)
6. **Head coach spotlight** — Nick Carpenter, 15+ yr experience, CTA to PT
7. **Photo gallery** — 9 images, no lightbox
8. **Pricing teaser** — 3 tiers with "Most Popular" badge, day-equivalent price, daily from LKR 3,500
9. **Testimonials** — 6 quote cards with photos
10. **Accommodation** — short-stay rooms, WhatsApp booking, "From LKR 7,500/night"
11. **Location / Find Us** — address, hours (every day 6:30 AM – 6:30 PM), amenities list, Google Maps link
12. **Footer** — Quick Links · Contact · Legal (T&Cs, Refund Policy, Privacy)

### Inner Pages

#### /schedule
- Weekly grid layout — days as columns, classes as rows within each day
- Day-by-day date picker with calendar; "Today" highlighted view
- Per-class info: time range, class name (bold), coach name, spot count ("10 left" / "Full"), thumbnail photo in Today view
- Live spot counts; "Free" badge for Loka Run Club; "Rest day" for non-class days
- "Book" CTAs route through login

#### /classes
- Two sections: Featured (4 highlighted) + full card grid (remaining 20)
- Cards: class name, 1–2 sentence description, "View Details" link
- **No discipline filters** — classes unsorted by category
- All classes described as "scalable for beginners to advanced"
- Disciplines span: Combat (Boxing, Kickboxing, Jiujitsu, BoxHIIT), Strength (Lower/Push/Pull/Full Body), Endurance (Hybrid, Run Club), Bodyweight (Calisthenics, Handstands), Movement/Dance (Hip Hop, House), Private

#### /pricing
- Duration toggle: 1 day · 3 days · 1 week · 2 weeks · 1 month · 10 drop-ins · 3 months · 6 months
- 3 tiers: **Loka Access** (gym + pool) · **Loka Performance** (unlimited classes, "most popular") · **Loka Lifestyle** (unlimited + recovery)
- Full comparison matrix for all passes × tiers
- Add-ons: Recovery packs (ice bath + sauna) — 1/3/5/10 session packs (LKR 3,500–25,000)
- Token packs: 1/3/5 class tokens (LKR 4,000–18,000), 15-day validity
- "Pass starts at first class" deferred-start messaging
- Buy buttons redirect to login → checkout

#### /personal-training
- Hero: "Your goals. Your trainer. Your results."
- 6 disciplines: Strength & Conditioning, Boxing, HIIT, Muay Thai, Kickboxing, Functional Fitness
- Solo / paired / small-group options
- No pricing table — WhatsApp contact direct with Nick (2× WhatsApp CTAs with pre-populated messages)
- No intake form or questionnaire

#### /about
- Founder narrative: beach circuits → garden gym → multi-site → permanent facility
- Mission: inclusive fitness community (travellers, nomads, locals)
- 4 values: Personalized · Community-Driven · Sustainable · Balanced
- Tone: authentic, aspirational, accessible, community-first
- Key quote: "I wanted Lokalanka to be more than a gym. It's about movement, mindset, and belonging."

#### /testimonials
- 7 quote cards with client photos
- No filters, no star ratings, no video testimonials
- Headline: "Real stories. Real results."
- Thematic variety: coaching quality, energy, facility, diverse classes, welcoming atmosphere
- CTA: "Ready to Start Your Own Story?"

#### /contact
- "Coach Inquiry" form: Name, Email, Company (honeypot?), Phone/WhatsApp (optional), Specialty dropdown, Experience, Open text
- Contact: email + WhatsApp + embedded Google Maps
- Hours: Every day 6:30 AM – 6:30 PM

### Design & UX Assessment

| Dimension | Rating | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Visual design | ★★★☆☆ | Clean, functional, dark-neutral; nothing distinctive |
| Motion / interaction | ★★☆☆☆ | Basic. No scroll animations, no custom cursor, no transitions |
| Information architecture | ★★★★☆ | Good structure; class grid could use filters |
| Mobile UX | ★★★☆☆ | Functional; no signature mobile patterns |
| Brand distinctiveness | ★★☆☆☆ | Generic gym aesthetic; nothing you'd remember |
| Performance | ★★★★☆ | Loads reasonably; no heavy WebGL |

### Gaps / Weaknesses (opportunities for TWH to own)
- No live schedule filters or heat-grid view
- No class discipline filters; no GSAP Flip transitions
- No custom cursor, no page transitions
- No cinematic hero video or WebGL
- No kinetic typography
- No command palette / global search
- Testimonials lack ratings, video, or filter by goal
- Gallery has no lightbox or filter
- Menu is standard dropdown — zero signature quality
- PT page lacks qualification questionnaire or package structure

## 2. makahiyafitness.com

### Overview
A boutique fitness hotel/retreat in Unawatuna, Sri Lanka. Package model: guests stay on-site and train. Core audience: fitness travellers seeking an all-in premium experience. Affiliated with HYROX.

### Navigation & Header
- Header: STAY · TRAIN · EAT · CLASSES · RECOVER · Packages · Cart · (no auth state visible)
- No announcement bar noted

### Home Page Flow (in order)
1. **Manifesto hero** — "WE ARE MAKAHIYA — THE BOUTIQUE FITNESS HOTEL" + "Fast-track your fitness." + "EXPLORE OUR PACKAGES" CTA
2. **Packages block** — 4 cards: B&B (£380, 2-night min), Signature (£1,055, 4-night min), Transformation (£5,600, 14-night min, "Limited Availability"), + HYROX affiliation banner
3. **Testimonials** — "WHAT OUR GUESTS SAY"
4. **FAQ accordion** — 6 questions with "More FAQs" CTA
5. **Community CTA** — "JOIN THE MAKAHIYA MASSIVE" + "Make the climb." + Get in touch + newsletter

### Inner Pages

#### /packages
- 3 tiers with comparison table: B&B · Signature · Transformation
- Transformation: "POA" pricing, requires questionnaire
- Storytelling framing: "Your Stay, Your Way" — personalisation emphasis
- Inclusive messaging: "complete beginners to seasoned athletes"
- External booking platform linked out (Acuity)

#### /classes
- Hero + intro copy
- 2 CTAs: "BUY A CLASS PASS" + "BOOK A CLASS" (both external Acuity links)
- "What to expect" section — community, inclusivity, coach walkthrough
- 4 lifestyle training photos
- "How to choose" section → Google Drive class info document
- No in-page schedule, no filter, no live availability

#### /faqs
- 27 questions total, accordion pattern
- Thematic groups: Location · Accommodation · Fitness & Training · Meals · Pricing & Value · Logistics
- No category tabs or search
- Brand voice: irreverent, first-person, sarcastic opener ("There are no stupid questions, only stupid answers"), TikTok jab, humour throughout
- Premium positioning addressed directly: "we're not the cheapest fitness holiday out there"
- Questions reveal inclusive positioning (age range, solo travellers, female trainers)

#### /gallery
- ~34 images, grid layout
- No filters, no lightbox (implied standard)
- No video content
- Captions reference specific offerings (bootcamp, gym, boutique rooms, food)
- Lifestyle narrative: training, recovery, facility, food, location

### Design & UX Assessment

| Dimension | Rating | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Visual design | ★★★☆☆ | Clean, hotel-inspired; photography-heavy |
| Motion / interaction | ★★☆☆☆ | Minimal; standard accordion + image cards |
| Information architecture | ★★★☆☆ | Logical but lean; booking offloaded to Acuity |
| Mobile UX | ★★★☆☆ | Functional; no signature patterns |
| Brand distinctiveness | ★★★★☆ | Stronger voice than Loka Lanka; irreverence works |
| Performance | ★★★★☆ | Standard; no heavy assets |

- Classes page has no in-page schedule or live availability
- Gallery has no filters, no lightbox, no video
- No page transitions or signature menu experience
- No kinetic typography or scroll-driven narrative

## 4. Key Insight

Neither reference site would win a design award. Both are functional and competent but visually forgettable. The gym/fitness vertical in Sri Lanka has **zero** examples of Awwwards-grade motion work, immersive WebGL, or signature brand experiences. This is The Wolverine Hub's primary competitive moat: not just matching the best gym sites in the country, but leapfrogging the entire category.
